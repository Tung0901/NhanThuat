## 1. Domain Purpose
The Conflict domain defines the structural dynamics of friction, disagreement, and opposing interests within organizations. It distinguishes cognitive task disagreement from affective relationship friction.

## 2. Core Distinctions
- **Task Conflict vs Relationship Conflict:** Task disagreement drives innovation; Relationship friction destroys psychological safety.
- **Conflict vs Escalation:** Conflict is tension; Escalation is emotional distortion.
