# Delegation Concept Map

The Delegation domain (NT-DA-0007) defines the structured transfer of capability from one node (leader) to another (follower) to enable autonomous execution.

## Core Ontology

- **Delegation**: The structured transfer of responsibility, authority, required information, resources, decision rights, and accountability necessary for autonomous execution.
- **Abdication**: Transferring responsibility without establishing monitoring loops or transferring required resources (often falsely labeled "empowerment").
- **Authority vs. Influence**: Authority is the structural right to make decisions and deploy resources; Influence is the emergent capacity to align behavior (NT-DA-0004). Delegation transfers authority, not influence.
- **Responsibility vs. Accountability**: Responsibility is the obligation to execute a task; Accountability is the ultimate ownership of the outcome.

## Conceptual Boundaries

- Differentiated from **Task Assignment**: Giving orders or assigning a list of chores is not delegation. Delegation involves transferring a boundary of autonomous decision-making.
- Differentiated from **Outsourcing**: Delegation happens within a shared accountability structure (the delegator retains ultimate accountability).
- Differentiated from **Leadership** (NT-DA-0004): Delegation is a structural mechanism that *consumes* Leadership capacity (trust and credibility).
