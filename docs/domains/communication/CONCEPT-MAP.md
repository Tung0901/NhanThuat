# Communication Concept Map

The Communication domain (NT-DA-0008) focuses strictly on the mechanics of information transfer between nodes, divorcing the transmission of data from the sociological impacts of influence or persuasion.

## Core Ontology

- **Transmission vs. Understanding**: Transmission is the physical delivery of a signal; Understanding is the successful, verified decoding of that signal's meaning by the receiver.
- **Interpretive Variance**: The inherent delta between the sender's semantic intent and the receiver's decoded meaning, driven by differing cognitive defaults (Personality) and contextual baselines.
- **Signal Degradation**: The natural loss of fidelity that occurs as information moves across nodes or time without active verification loops.

## Conceptual Boundaries

- Differentiated from **Influence/Leadership**: Communication is the *vehicle*. Leadership is the *engine*. Communication ensures the message arrived; it does not ensure the receiver agreed to act on it.
- Differentiated from **Conflict Resolution**: Disagreement over goals is a structural reality, not a "communication failure." Talking more clearly will not resolve a zero-sum conflict of interest.
- Differentiated from **Charisma**: Communication relies on structural confirmation loops, not emotional arousal or presentation skills.
