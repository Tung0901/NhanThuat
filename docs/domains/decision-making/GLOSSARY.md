# Decision Making Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0015  
**Slug:** decision-making  
**Status:** ready_for_review  

### Analysis Paralysis
The inability to make a decision due to over-thinking, over-analyzing, or demanding unattainable levels of certainty.

### Decision
An irrevocable allocation of resources (time, money, effort) toward a selected alternative, made under uncertainty. A choice without commitment is not a decision.

### Decision Quality
A measure of the rigor, evidence-gathering, and logic used during the decision-making process, completely independent of the actual outcome.

### Evidence
Information specifically evaluated and applied to reduce uncertainty regarding a set of choices or a hypothesis. 

### Opportunity Cost
The value of the next best alternative that is forgone as a result of making a decision. Every decision carries an opportunity cost.

### Outcome Bias
The cognitive failure to evaluate the quality of a decision solely based on its final result, ignoring the role of luck or unknown variables at the time the decision was made.

### Result Quality
The objective goodness or badness of the final outcome. Good decisions can result in poor result quality due to variance and luck.

### Risk
A state where the probability of various outcomes is known or can be reliably estimated.

### Uncertainty
A state where the probability of outcomes is unknown, unquantifiable, or deeply ambiguous. Uncertainty can be reduced through evidence but never completely eliminated.
