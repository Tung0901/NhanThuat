# Decision Making Concept Map

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0015  
**Slug:** decision-making  
**Status:** ready_for_review

## 1. Domain Overview

**Scope:** The structured process of evaluating alternatives under uncertainty and selecting a course of action.  
**Out of Scope:** Problem identification (Problem Solving), action coordination (Execution), group consensus mechanics (Voting).

## 2. Core Distinctions

To maintain rigor, this domain insists on the following conceptual boundaries:

| Concept | Versus | Concept | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Decision** | vs | **Outcome** | A decision is the choice made based on available information. The outcome includes luck and variables outside of control. |
| **Decision Quality** | vs | **Result Quality** | Good decisions can have bad outcomes; bad decisions can have good outcomes. Evaluation must focus on the process. |
| **Risk** | vs | **Uncertainty** | Risk involves known probabilities. Uncertainty involves unknown or unquantifiable probabilities. |
| **Choice** | vs | **Decision** | A choice is selecting an option; a decision requires an irrevocable commitment of resources. |
| **Information** | vs | **Evidence** | Information is raw data. Evidence is information applied to reduce uncertainty about a specific hypothesis or alternative. |
| **Probability** | vs | **Certainty** | Decisions are probabilistic. Demanding absolute certainty before deciding is a structural failure (Analysis Paralysis). |

## 3. Concept Clusters

### 3.1 Evaluating Under Uncertainty
*   **Definitions:** How to measure what is unknown and establish a boundary for acceptable risk.
*   **Core Concepts:** Irreducible Uncertainty, Evidence-Risk tradeoff, Probability.
*   **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0049, NT-PRINCIPLE-0079, NT-MODEL-0017.

### 3.2 Decision Quality & Evaluation
*   **Definitions:** How to decouple the quality of a decision process from its ultimate outcome.
*   **Core Concepts:** Decision Quality Independence, Outcome Bias, Process Evaluation.
*   **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0048, NT-PRINCIPLE-0078, NT-MODEL-0015, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0023.

### 3.3 Choice and Opportunity Cost
*   **Definitions:** The economic and cognitive reality that choosing one path eliminates others.
*   **Core Concepts:** Opportunity Cost, Tradeoffs.
*   **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0050, NT-PRINCIPLE-0080.

### 3.4 Cognitive Failure Modes
*   **Definitions:** Systematic biases that prevent high-quality decisions from being made.
*   **Core Concepts:** False Certainty, Analysis Paralysis, Authority Bias, Decision by Popularity.
*   **Knowledge Units:** NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0024, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0025, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0026, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0027.
