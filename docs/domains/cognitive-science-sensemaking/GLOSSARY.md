# Cognitive Science & Sensemaking Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0026  
**Slug:** cognitive-science-sensemaking  
**Status:** review

* **Attention is Finite and Directional:** Attention is a strictly limited resource that can only be directed sequentially, making multitasking a myth of rapid context-switching with high switching costs
* **Memory Reconstruction:** Memory is not a recording retrieved from storage, but a narrative reconstructed at the moment of recall, heavily influenced by present context and emotion
* **Information Processing Pipeline:** A structural model of how humans perceive, filter, process, and act upon environmental information
* **Sensemaking Loop:** The iterative process by which individuals and groups structure the unknown to enable action
* **Metacognitive Regulation Loop:** The structural model of how humans monitor, evaluate, and adjust their own cognitive processes and strategies during complex tasks
* **Illusion of Explanatory Depth:** The false belief that one understands a complex system deeply, which collapses only when forced to explain the mechanics step-by-step
* **Confirmation Bias Trap:** The tendency to eagerly accept information that confirms prior beliefs while rigidly scrutinizing or ignoring disconfirming evidence
* **Availability Heuristic Fallacy:** Estimating the frequency or probability of an event based on how easily examples come to mind, leading to massive risk miscalculation
* **Overconfidence Illusion:** The structural tendency for individuals to be more confident in their judgments and predictions than their objective accuracy warrants
* **Ambiguity Aversion:** Humans consistently prefer known risks over unknown uncertainties, often choosing inferior options to avoid ambiguity
* **Anchoring Effect:** The first piece of information offered in a decision context exerts a disproportionate influence on all subsequent judgments and estimates
* **Metacognitive Incompetence Illusion:** Humans are inherently poor at accurately evaluating their own incompetence, because the skills required to perform a task are the exact same skills required to evaluate performance on that task
* **Optimize Cognitive Load:** Design environments, communications, and processes to minimize extraneous cognitive load, freeing capacity for intrinsic problem-solving
* **Explicit Mental Modeling:** Require individuals and teams to make their underlying assumptions and mental models explicit before making strategic decisions or resolving conflicts
* **Foster Metacognitive Reflection:** Design workflows to include mandatory structural pauses that force teams and individuals to step out of execution and evaluate their cognitive approach
* **De-bias through Structured Evaluation:** Do not rely on willpower or awareness to overcome cognitive bias; instead, build structured environments and checklists that force objective evaluation
