# Leadership Concept Map

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0004  
**Slug:** leadership  
**Status:** ready_for_review  

## Core Question

How does effective, ethically legitimate leadership emerge from the interaction of leader behaviors, follower agency, shared purpose, and situational constraints, and how does it mobilize voluntary effort without relying on coercion or manipulation?

## Ontological Chain: Influence to Alignment

To model leadership dynamically and avoid hierarchical fallates:

*   **Credibility Basis:** Earned trust, competence, and consistency form the bedrock of personal credibility.
*   **Influence Attempt:** The translation of personal credibility and shared purpose into direction-setting communication, adapted to recipient traits.
*   **Follower Agency / Alignment:** Follower alignment emerges when agency is respected, leading to voluntary commitment rather than compliance.
*   **Collective Action:** The resulting coordinated effort directed toward the shared purpose, mediated by situational constraints.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Leadership builds upon upstream foundations in:
- **Human Nature (`NT-DA-0001`):** Psychological safety, dignity, status, and threat/reward.
- **Motivation (`NT-DA-0002`):** Autonomy, value, feasibility, and identity-driven persistence.
- **Personality (`NT-DA-0003`):** Trait preferences, adaptation costs, and situation strength constraints.

Leadership provides assumptions and guidelines for downstream areas:
- **Delegation (`NT-DA-0007`):** Trust and execution boundaries.
- **Team Building (`NT-DA-0006`):** Designing safe, collaborative units.
- **Culture (`NT-DA-0019`):** Cultivating shared values and norms.

## Structured Cluster Records

### LDR-CL-001 — authority-versus-influence

- **Definition:** The boundary between structural role power and earned personal credibility.
- **In scope:** Personal credibility dynamics, management vs. leadership tasks, formal vs. informal leadership, role power limits.
- **Out of scope:** Detailed delegation workflows.
- **Core questions:** Why is formal authority insufficient for adaptive change? How is personal credibility built and spent?
- **Candidate references:** NT-LAW-0037, NT-PRINCIPLE-0063, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0012.

### LDR-CL-002 — trust-versus-obedience

- **Definition:** The distinction between trust-based alignment and coercion-driven compliance.
- **In scope:** Trust accumulation, reciprocal vulnerability, compliance safety, coercive pressure markers.
- **Out of scope:** General contract negotiation or legal agreements.
- **Core questions:** How does coercion erode psychological safety? How can leaders distinguish compliance from trust?
- **Candidate references:** NT-LAW-0038, NT-PRINCIPLE-0064, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0013.

### LDR-CL-003 — adaptive-leadership-behavior

- **Definition:** How leadership behavior adapts to situation strength, task complexity, and group dynamics.
- **In scope:** Situational strength modulation, style adaptation, cognitive load management in groups, personality-independent competence.
- **Out of scope:** General skill building curricula.
- **Core questions:** How should a leader adapt execution support without being seen as inconsistent?
- **Candidate references:** NT-LAW-0039, NT-PRINCIPLE-0065, NT-MODEL-0009.

### LDR-CL-004 — ethical-legitimacy-and-safeguards

- **Definition:** The boundaries preventing manipulation and establishing ethical legitimacy.
- **In scope:** Manipulation vs. influence criteria, ethical safeguards, dissent protection, transparency frameworks.
- **Out of scope:** General corporate compliance policies.
- **Core questions:** How does a leader verify that their influence is ethically legitimate? What safeguards protect dissent?
- **Candidate references:** NT-MODEL-0010, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0014.
