# Team Building Glossary

- **Cognitive Diversity:** Variance in how individuals perceive, process, and act upon information, driven by underlying trait defaults and experiences.
- **Structural Interdependence:** A workflow design where the output of one individual is required as the input for another, or where a goal cannot be achieved without coordinated, simultaneous effort.
- **Clone Factory:** The anti-pattern of composing teams entirely of individuals with similar trait profiles to minimize initial friction, resulting in massive blind spots.
