**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0001

**Status:** frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Review owner:** Product Owner or delegated Human Nature domain reviewer

## Objective

The Human Nature domain foundation defines reusable knowledge about recurring
human tendencies, needs, limits, biases, and social behavior. It directly
anchors later domain work in Motivation, Personality, Communication, Influence,
Leadership, and Ethics, with transitive relevance for Authority, Decision
Making, Trust, Conflict, Learning, and Culture.

## Boundaries

Human Nature covers durable tendencies of human perception, energy, identity,
attention, emotion, belonging, meaning, and adaptation. It does not implement
deep domain content for Motivation, Personality, Hiring, Negotiation, Strategy,
or Execution. Those areas may depend on Human Nature, but they remain separate
domain expansions.

## Evidence Posture

All new units use provisional inline evidence with empty references. This is
intentional: the domain foundation records structured reviewed knowledge without
fabricating external citations. Future evidence batches may add standalone
evidence records and link them through the Evidence Layer.
