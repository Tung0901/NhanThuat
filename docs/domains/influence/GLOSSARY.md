# Influence Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0014  
**Slug:** influence  
**Status:** frozen  

### Agency Preservation
The core condition of legitimate influence where the target actor retains full voluntary freedom and informed choice to accept or reject a proposed direction.

### Coercive Leverage
The misuse of resource dominance, threat, or penalty to force compliance. Coercive leverage is the anti-pattern opposite of legitimate influence.

### Decision Bridge
The architectural role of influence in converting clear communication and structural trust into concrete decision evaluation and execution commitment.

### Intent Transparency
The explicit, unhidden disclosure of a decision-maker's or influencer's goals, eliminating manipulative information asymmetry.

### Legitimate Influence
The capability to shift an actor's beliefs, priorities, or behaviors through value reframing and reason while preserving agency and intent transparency.

### Manipulative Framing
The practice of distorting context, concealing critical data, or exploiting emotional vulnerabilities to trick an actor into a choice they would otherwise reject.

### Reframing
Changing the cognitive context or perspective through which a situation or option is evaluated, altering its perceived value without distorting facts.

### Value Alignment
The state where a proposed action or commitment is demonstrated to directly support the intrinsic goals, identity, or shared purpose of the target actor.
