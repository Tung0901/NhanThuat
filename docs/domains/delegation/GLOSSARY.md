# Delegation Glossary

- **Delegation:** The structured transfer of responsibility, authority, required information, resources, decision rights, and accountability necessary for autonomous execution.
- **Abdication:** The dangerous anti-pattern of transferring responsibility without structural monitoring loops or symmetric resource transfer, often disguised as "empowerment."
- **Symmetrical Transfer:** The absolute requirement that any transfer of responsibility must be matched with an equal and corresponding transfer of authority, resources, and information.
- **Retained Accountability:** The principle that while a leader can delegate the responsibility to execute a task, they cannot delegate the ultimate accountability for the success or failure of that task.
