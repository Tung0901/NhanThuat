# Communication Glossary

- **Transmission:** The act of sending a signal (email, speech, document) across a medium.
- **Confirmation:** The structural feedback loop where the receiver proves they have accurately decoded the sender's semantic intent.
- **Interpretive Variance:** The inevitable gap between what the sender meant and what the receiver understood, caused by differing contexts and traits.
- **Broadcast Fallacy:** The dangerous assumption that hitting "send" or making an announcement equates to successful communication.
- **Semantic Illusion:** The false belief that because two people use the same vocabulary word, they share the exact same conceptual definition.
