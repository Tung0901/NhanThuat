# Human Nature Concept Map

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0001  
**Slug:** human-nature  
**Status:** frozen

## Core Question

What recurring human tendencies must Nhan Thuat understand before designing
motivation, leadership, communication, authority, culture, learning, and
execution systems?

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Human Nature provides upstream assumptions for:

- Motivation: needs, energy, attention, meaning, and identity.
- Personality: stable differences in sensitivity, preference, and adaptation.
- Communication: perception, defense, candor, and interpretation.
- Influence: attention, status, belonging, and framing.
- Leadership: safety, standards, dignity, and commitment.
- Ethics: dignity, manipulation risk, fairness, and legitimate use of people.

Needs and biases are approved Human Nature scope but are foundation-level in this
wave. Needs appear through meaning, belonging, identity, energy, and safety.
Biases appear through attention, salience, defense, identity filtering, and load.
Dedicated needs/biases expansion is deferred to a later Human Nature evidence or
model batch.

## Completeness Criteria

This foundation is ready for review when:

- every concept cluster has at least one law or model and one operational
  principle;
- cross-cutting guardrails may be anti-pattern-only when their purpose is to
  constrain misuse rather than define a standalone concept cluster;
- anti-patterns capture common misuse of Human Nature concepts;
- all new relation targets resolve;
- evidence is explicitly provisional where external sources do not yet exist;
- no content outside Human Nature is generated;
- validation, tests, and ruff pass.

## Structured Cluster Records

### HN-CL-001 — attention-and-meaning

- Definition: how interpreted significance directs limited attention.
- In scope: salience, meaning, relevance, attention loops.
- Out of scope: persuasion tactics and influence campaigns.
- Core questions: What makes a signal noticeable? How does meaning shape
  attention?
- Existing-unit references: NT-LAW-0002, NT-PRINCIPLE-0044, NT-MODEL-0001.
- Candidate gaps: external evidence on attention and salience.
- Evidence needs: behavioral science sources on attention allocation.
- Coverage disposition: sufficient for foundation review.

### HN-CL-002 — emotion-and-action

- Definition: how emotion prepares interpretation, body, attention, and likely
  response.
- In scope: emotion as action readiness, feeling/fact separation, response
  choice.
- Out of scope: clinical emotion treatment or therapy methods.
- Core questions: What does emotion prepare a person to do? How can emotion be
  respected without surrendering judgment?
- Existing-unit references: NT-LAW-0024, NT-PRINCIPLE-0046.
- Candidate gaps: evidence on affect, appraisal, and action tendency.
- Evidence needs: reviewed behavioral science sources on emotion and action.
- Coverage disposition: sufficient for foundation review.

### HN-CL-003 — safety-and-defense

- Definition: how perceived threat narrows openness, truth flow, and learning.
- In scope: threat, defense, safety, candor, truth under threat.
- Out of scope: formal security policy and legal investigations.
- Core questions: When does threat block truth? What safety is needed for
  learning without lowering standards?
- Existing-unit references: NT-LAW-0025, NT-PRINCIPLE-0045,
  NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0001.
- Candidate gaps: stronger evidence on psychological safety and accountability.
- Evidence needs: organizational research on safety, candor, and reporting.
- Coverage disposition: sufficient for foundation review.

### HN-CL-004 — identity-and-status

- Definition: how identity, belonging, competence, and status filter change and
  interpretation.
- In scope: identity defense, belonging, status sensitivity, resistance
  diagnosis.
- Out of scope: full Personality domain classification.
- Core questions: What identity is being protected? How does belonging shape
  response to change?
- Existing-unit references: NT-LAW-0014, NT-LAW-0026, NT-PRINCIPLE-0047,
  NT-PRINCIPLE-0048, NT-MODEL-0002.
- Candidate gaps: evidence on identity threat and group belonging.
- Evidence needs: social psychology and organizational identity sources.
- Coverage disposition: sufficient for foundation review.

### HN-CL-005 — energy-and-load

- Definition: how cognitive, emotional, and physical load reduce regulation,
  patience, and judgment.
- In scope: cognitive load, energy, body signals, self-regulation.
- Out of scope: full Execution throughput or performance-management systems.
- Core questions: How does load change behavior? Which body signals reveal load
  before failure?
- Existing-unit references: NT-LAW-0015, NT-LAW-0027, NT-PRINCIPLE-0049,
  NT-PRINCIPLE-0050, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0002.
- Candidate gaps: external evidence on cognitive load and somatic load signals.
- Evidence needs: learning science and behavioral decision sources.
- Coverage disposition: sufficient for foundation review.

### HN-CL-006 — adaptation-and-learning

- Definition: how repeated consequences and context shape expectation, habit,
  learning, and cultural adaptation.
- In scope: adaptation cycle, repeated consequence, accidental conditioning.
- Out of scope: full Learning domain curriculum or Culture domain expansion.
- Core questions: What is the system teaching repeatedly? When is adaptation
  being mistaken for agreement?
- Existing-unit references: NT-LAW-0028, NT-PRINCIPLE-0051, NT-MODEL-0003,
  NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0003.
- Candidate gaps: evidence on reinforcement, habit formation, and culture.
- Evidence needs: behavioral learning and organizational culture sources.
- Coverage disposition: sufficient for foundation review.

  of behavioral knowledge.

  manipulation risk.
