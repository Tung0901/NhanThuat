# Trust Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0010  
**Slug:** trust  
**Status:** ready_for_review  

### Affective Trust
An interpersonal bond based on emotional closeness, personal sympathy, or friendship. In NhanThuat, affective trust is explicitly distinguished from structural organizational trust.

### Calibrated Trust
The precise alignment between the degree of vulnerability granted to an actor and their empirical, verified competence and reliability within a specific boundary.

### Control
An enforcement mechanism relying on continuous monitoring, active surveillance, and restrictive rules to compel compliance. Control is the structural inverse of trust.

### Credibility
The verifiable record of an actor's past performance, competence, and adherence to commitments. Credibility is past-oriented evidence used to calibrate future trust.

### Psychological Safety
The shared belief that a team environment is safe for interpersonal risk-taking (speaking up, admitting mistakes). Psychological safety creates the environment in which candid communication occurs.

### Structural Trust
An organizational efficiency mechanism that replaces active surveillance and redundant verification with the expected, verified reliability of an actor under vulnerability, directly reducing coordination costs.

### Trust Breach
A structural failure where an actor fails to deliver on a critical commitment or exceeds assigned boundaries under vulnerability, dramatically increasing future verification costs.

### Verification Cadence
The structured, non-surveillance schedule of periodic spot-checks and milestone reviews used to maintain calibrated trust without degrading into micromanagement.
