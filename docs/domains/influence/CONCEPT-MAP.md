# Influence Concept Map

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0014  
**Slug:** influence  
**Status:** frozen

## 1. Domain Overview

**Scope:** Legitimate mechanisms for shifting beliefs, priorities, and choices while maintaining actor agency and intent transparency.  
**Out of Scope:** Positional command (Authority), coercive force (Power), deceptive tricks (Manipulation), rhetoric in isolation (Persuasion), financial payoffs (Incentives), and continuous surveillance (Control).

## 2. Core Distinctions Matrix

| Concept | Versus | Concept | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Influence** | vs | **Authority** | Influence shifts choices voluntarily; Authority mandates compliance by structural right. |
| **Influence** | vs | **Power** | Influence relies on shared value frames; Power relies on resource dominance or threat. |
| **Influence** | vs | **Manipulation** | Influence maintains transparent intent and agency; Manipulation hides intent and exploits informational asymmetry. |
| **Influence** | vs | **Persuasion** | Influence is systemic execution bridge capability; Persuasion is localized communicative framing. |
| **Influence** | vs | **Leadership** | Leadership sets the destination; Influence aligns individual choices to walk the path. |
| **Influence** | vs | **Negotiation** | Influence alters perceived value before exchange; Negotiation trades concessions during exchange. |
| **Influence** | vs | **Incentives** | Influence shapes intrinsic commitment; Incentives apply extrinsic payoffs. |
| **Influence** | vs | **Control** | Influence induces voluntary self-governance; Control enforces external compliance. |

## 3. Concept Clusters

### 3.1 Legitimate Mechanism & Value Framing
- **Definitions:** How influence operates transparently by reframing significance and aligning values.
- **Core Concepts:** Transparent framing, value alignment, voluntary commitment.
- **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0054, NT-PRINCIPLE-0084, NT-PRINCIPLE-0086.

### 3.2 The Decision Bridge Pipeline
- **Definitions:** Structuring the flow from Communication through Trust to Decision Making and Execution.
- **Core Concepts:** Decision bridge, commitment shift, execution readiness.
- **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0055, NT-PRINCIPLE-0085, NT-MODEL-0020, NT-MODEL-0021.

### 3.3 Pathological Failures & Counter-measures
- **Definitions:** Diagnosing deceptive, coercive, or isolated persuasion failures that degrade trust.
- **Core Concepts:** Influence degradation, manipulative framing, coercive leverage, pseudo-consensus.
- **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0056, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0032, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0033, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0034, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0035.
