# Trust Concept Map

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0010  
**Slug:** trust  
**Status:** ready_for_review

## 1. Domain Overview

**Scope:** Structural mechanisms reducing organizational transaction/coordination costs by substituting expected reliability under vulnerability for active surveillance.  
**Out of Scope:** Affective bonding, personal feelings of closeness, unconditional loyalty, public relations management, and legal contract enforcement.

## 2. Core Distinctions Matrix

| Concept | Versus | Concept | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trust** | vs | **Credibility** | Trust is future-oriented willingness to accept vulnerability; Credibility is past-oriented empirical evidence of competence. |
| **Trust** | vs | **Psychological Safety** | Safety creates freedom to express vulnerability without fear of penalty; Trust relies on expected competence to deliver specific outcomes. |
| **Trust** | vs | **Friendship** | Friendship is emotional affinity; Trust is structural execution alignment. |
| **Trust** | vs | **Loyalty** | Loyalty is uncritical allegiance to an entity; Trust is calibrated, conditional reliance bounded by performance and verification. |
| **Trust** | vs | **Reputation** | Reputation is macro public perception; Trust is micro/meso structural expectation. |
| **Trust** | vs | **Control** | Control enforces compliance via continuous monitoring; Trust replaces monitoring with verified reliability within agreed boundaries. |

## 3. Concept Clusters

### 3.1 Structural Trust Mechanics
- **Definitions:** Trust as an economic and structural engine that reduces friction and transaction overhead.
- **Core Concepts:** Coordination cost reduction, vulnerability acceptance, agency allocation.
- **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0051, NT-PRINCIPLE-0081, NT-PRINCIPLE-0083.

### 3.2 Calibration & Verification Boundaries
- **Definitions:** Designing structural verification cadences without degrading trust into control or surveillance.
- **Core Concepts:** Calibrated trust, verification boundaries, competence alignment.
- **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0052, NT-PRINCIPLE-0082, NT-MODEL-0018, NT-MODEL-0019.

### 3.3 Systemic Failure Modes & Breach Recovery
- **Definitions:** Identifying misattributions where trust is confused with emotion, control, or loyalty, and modeling structural breach repair.
- **Core Concepts:** Breach repair asymmetry, surveillance paradox, affective illusion, loyalty trap.
- **Knowledge Units:** NT-LAW-0053, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0028, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0029, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0030, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0031.
