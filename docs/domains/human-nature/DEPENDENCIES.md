# Human Nature Cross-Domain Dependency Mapping

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0001  
**Status:** frozen

## Upstream Dependencies

Human Nature is the first Wave 1 production domain and has no domain-area
authoring prerequisites.

## Downstream Domain Areas

| Target domain area | Dependency kind | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NT-DA-0002 motivation | authoring_prerequisite | Motivation depends on needs, meaning, identity, load, and energy. |
| NT-DA-0003 personality | authoring_prerequisite | Personality work needs a baseline of shared human tendencies before differences are classified. |
| NT-DA-0013 communication | authoring_prerequisite | Communication depends on attention, interpretation, safety, and defense. |
| NT-DA-0014 influence | authoring_prerequisite | Influence uses attention, status, belonging, framing, and meaning. |
| NT-DA-0004 leadership | authoring_prerequisite | Leadership depends on dignity, safety, standards, motivation, and human limits. |
| NT-DA-0020 ethics | conceptual_foundation | Ethical use of people depends on dignity, vulnerability, and manipulation risk. |

Authority, Decision Making, Trust, Conflict, Learning, and Culture are transitive
downstream consumers through the direct dependencies above.

## Existing Unit Links

Human Nature extends existing Batch 1 concepts on context, status, identity,
energy, habits, uncertainty, participation, and learning safety without
modifying frozen artifacts.
