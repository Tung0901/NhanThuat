# Hiring Concept Map

The Hiring domain (NT-DA-0005) focuses on the structured acquisition of human capability, recognizing the inherent limits of predicting human behavior across shifting contexts.

## Core Ontology

- **Signal Distortion**: High-stakes environments (interviews) distort natural behavioral expressions into socially desirable signals.
- **Predictive Limits**: Past behavior only predicts future behavior if the systemic context remains relatively constant.
- **Structural Fit**: Evaluating candidates against the precise demands of the role rather than abstract notions of "culture."
- **Heuristic Bias**: The natural human tendency to substitute complex evaluations with simple, flawed proxies (e.g., "likeability" or "intuition").

## Conceptual Boundaries

- Differentiated from **Personality** (which studies underlying traits; hiring focuses on measuring specific behavioral expressions).
- Differentiated from **Team-Building** (which focuses on integrating members post-hire).
