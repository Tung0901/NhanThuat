# Leadership Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0004  
**Slug:** leadership  
**Status:** ready_for_review  

*   **Adaptive leadership behavior:** The flexible modulation of influence styles, environments, and situational strength based on follower agency, task complexity, and context, rather than relying on a single fixed style.
*   **Authority:** The formal, structural right to command compliance and exercise power associated with a position or role, independent of personal credibility.
*   **Coercion:** The practice of forcing compliance through implicit or explicit threat, pressure, or penalties, which violates follower agency and destroys trust.
*   **Ethical legitimacy:** The moral alignment of leadership intent, means, and outcomes with the dignity and agency of followers, transforming raw power into accepted authority.
*   **Influence:** The capacity to affect the behavior, values, or beliefs of others through credibility, reasoning, and relationship, without relying on structural coercion or manipulation.
*   **Leadership:** The process of social influence, emerging from mutual trust and credibility, oriented toward a shared purpose. It is distinct from management, formal authority, and mere popularity or charisma.
*   **Manipulation:** The covert, deceptive, or exploitative direction of others' attention, emotion, or behavior, bypassing their conscious agency for the influencer's sole benefit.
*   **Obedience:** Compliant behavior in response to formal authority or coercive power, occurring independently of genuine trust or shared purpose.
*   **Personal credibility:** The earned trust, respect, and perceived competence that enables an individual to influence others voluntarily.
*   **Role power:** The coercive or reward-based leverage granted to an individual by a formal position.
*   **Trust:** The voluntary willingness of followers to align with a leader's direction, based on expectations of consistency, credibility, competence, and benevolence.
