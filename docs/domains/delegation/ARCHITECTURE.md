# Delegation Domain Architecture

## Scope

The Delegation domain establishes the mechanics of transferring execution capability while maintaining structural integrity. It defines the symmetries required for autonomous work to succeed.

## Ethical Safeguards

Delegation must protect followers from being set up to fail. Transferring accountability or responsibility without the symmetric transfer of authority and resources is an unethical structural failure (The Authority Mirage). Monitoring must be transparent and agreed upon to avoid the psychological damage of micromanagement.

## Design Decisions

- Strictly separates *Accountability* (retained by the delegator) from *Responsibility* (transferred to the delegatee).
- Defines "monitoring" as a necessary component of delegation, distinguishing it from "micromanagement" through the use of predefined structural feedback loops.
