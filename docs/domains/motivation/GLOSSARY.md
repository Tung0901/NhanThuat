# Motivation Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0002  
**Slug:** motivation  
**Status:** frozen

- **Autonomy:** the degree of discretion and self-determination an individual exercises over their choices, methods, and actions.
- **Competence / Mastery:** the internal drive to develop capability, exercise craft skill, and experience effectiveness in problem solving.
- **Crowding-out effect:** the phenomenon where adding heavy extrinsic rewards or monitoring reduces intrinsic motivation and internal task ownership.
- **Demotivation:** the active decline or loss of effort willingness resulting from perceived low value, low feasibility, high friction, or unacknowledged work.
- **Effort:** the voluntary allocation of cognitive, emotional, and physical energy toward achieving a goal.
- **Expectancy:** the subjective probability that individual effort will lead to successful task execution.
- **Extrinsic motivation:** drive originating from external consequences, such as monetary rewards, status gain, recognition, or threat of penalty.
- **Feasibility:** the perceived degree to which an intended goal or action can be practically accomplished given available resources, skills, and constraints.
- **Friction:** procedural, technical, administrative, or cognitive hurdles that increase the effort cost of completing an action.
- **Identity fit:** the degree of alignment between a task or role demand and an individual's self-concept, personal values, and professional standards.
- **Intrinsic motivation:** drive originating from internal interest, inherent task satisfaction, curiosity, craft pride, or personal enjoyment.
- **Progress momentum:** the accelerating psychological energy generated by visible evidence of incremental milestone completion.
- **Self-efficacy:** an individual's belief in their capability to execute actions required to produce specific performance attainments.
- **Threat-driven urgency:** short-term energy mobilized by perceived danger, fear of punishment, or social risk, aimed at avoiding negative consequences.
- **Value:** the perceived worth, significance, or desirability attributed to a goal outcome.
- **Willpower:** the finite conscious cognitive capacity to override distraction, fatigue, or demotivation to sustain focus on a goal.
