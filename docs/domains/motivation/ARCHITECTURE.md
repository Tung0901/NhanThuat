# Motivation Domain Architecture

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0002  
**Slug:** motivation  
**Status:** frozen  
**Wave:** Milestone 2 Wave 1  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Review owner:** Product Owner or delegated Motivation domain reviewer

## Objective

The Motivation domain foundation defines reusable knowledge about the drivers of human effort, intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, identity-driven persistence, progress feedback, threat dynamics, and willpower limits. It builds upon upstream Human Nature foundations (`NT-DA-0001`) and provides stewardship for downstream domains such as Incentives, Leadership, Learning, Hiring, and Delegation.

## Boundaries

Motivation covers the psychological and operational mechanics that govern voluntary effort allocation, task commitment, reward dynamics, and progress feedback loops. It does not implement deep domain content for Incentives (`NT-DA-0009`), Leadership (`NT-DA-0004`), or Execution (`NT-DA-0017`), which remain separate domain expansions.

## Taxonomy Placement

- Primary top-level domain: `tu-than`
- Secondary top-level domains: `tri-nhan`, `dung-nhan`

Primary domain stewardship implies responsibility for review, consistency, and organizational alignment without claiming exclusive conceptual ownership.

## Evidence Posture

All new units utilize provisional inline evidence with empty reference lists. This maintains backward compatibility and avoids fabricating unreviewed external citations. Standalone evidence records will be linked in future evidence maturation iterations via the Evidence Layer.
