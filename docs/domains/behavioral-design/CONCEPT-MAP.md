# Behavioral Design Concept Map

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0028  
**Slug:** behavioral-design  
**Status:** review

## Core Question

How can products, processes, and environments be designed to steer behavior reliably and ethically?

## Unit Inventory

*   **NT-MODEL-3201 — Habit Loop (Cue-Routine-Reward)** (Model)
    - A neurological loop that governs any habit, consisting of a cue that triggers the brain, a routine (the behavior itself), and a reward.
*   **NT-MODEL-3202 — Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP)** (Model)
    - Behavior (B) happens when three elements converge at the same moment: Motivation (M), Ability (A), and a Prompt (P).
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3201 — Goal Gradient Effect** (Phenomenon)
    - As people get closer to a goal, their efforts accelerate.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3202 — Zeigarnik Effect** (Phenomenon)
    - People remember uncompleted or interrupted tasks better than completed tasks.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3203 — Variable Reward Schedule** (Phenomenon)
    - Rewards delivered on an unpredictable, variable ratio schedule are highly effective at maintaining continuous behavior.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3204 — Temptation Bundling** (Phenomenon)
    - Combining a behavior that is good for you in the long run with a behavior that feels good in the short run.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3205 — Decision Fatigue (Ego Depletion)** (Phenomenon)
    - The deteriorating quality of decisions made by an individual after a long session of decision making.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3206 — Implementation Intentions** (Phenomenon)
    - A self-regulatory strategy in the form of an 'if-then' plan that leads to better goal attainment.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3207 — Overjustification Effect (Extrinsic Crowding-Out)** (Phenomenon)
    - Providing an external reward for an activity that was already intrinsically rewarding decreases a person's intrinsic motivation to perform it.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3208 — Pre-commitment** (Phenomenon)
    - Making a binding decision in advance when in a cold, rational state to prevent acting irrationally in a future hot state.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3201 — Commitment Devices** (Principle)
    - Voluntarily constraining future choices to prevent oneself from acting against long-term interests.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3202 — Goal Shielding** (Principle)
    - Actively inhibiting alternative goals or distractions to protect the focal goal.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3203 — Environment Design for Habituation** (Principle)
    - Modifying physical or digital spaces to make good behaviors obvious and easy, and bad behaviors invisible and difficult.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3204 — Minimum Viable Routine (Tiny Habits)** (Principle)
    - Scaling down a desired new behavior to an absurdly small, easily executable version to guarantee consistency.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3205 — Action Prompting (Contextual Triggers)** (Principle)
    - Anchoring a new habit to an existing, reliable habit rather than relying on time or memory.
