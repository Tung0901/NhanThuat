**Status:** frozen

## 1. Domain Purpose
The Ethics domain defines the structural safeguards, moral risk protections, and normative boundaries that ensure organizational power and technology serve human flourishing without harm or extraction.

## 2. Core Distinctions
- **Ethics vs Compliance:** Compliance follows legal regulations; Ethics ensures moral integrity even when laws are silent.
- **Ethics vs Morality:** Morality is personal belief; Ethics is systematic organizational governance.
