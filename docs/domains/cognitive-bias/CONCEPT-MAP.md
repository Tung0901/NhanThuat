# Cognitive Bias Concept Map

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0023  
**Slug:** cognitive-bias  
**Status:** review

## Core Question

How do systematic distortions in judgment and perception degrade decisions, and how can structures guard against them?

## Concept Clusters

| Cluster | Scope | Units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cognitive-bias-laws | Foundational statements about cognitive-bias defaults and mechanisms. | NT-LAW-2301 |
| cognitive-bias-principles | Operational rules translating cognitive-bias understanding into action. | NT-PRINCIPLE-2301 |
| cognitive-bias-models | Structured representations of cognitive-bias mechanisms and interventions. | NT-MODEL-2301 |
| cognitive-bias-anti-patterns | Common failures and misuse of cognitive-bias concepts. | NT-ANTI-PATTERN-2301 |

## Completeness Criteria

This domain foundation is complete and ready for review when:
- Every concept cluster references at least one knowledge unit;
- Phenomena document observable patterns with conditions and exceptions;
- Principles are actionable and reference supporting units where relevant;
- Anti-patterns (where present) document misuse and warning signs;
- Validation and tests pass.
