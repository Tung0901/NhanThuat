## Structured Cluster Records

### MOT-CL-001 — value-and-feasibility

- Definition: How effort decisions balance expected value, feasibility, and friction.
- In scope: Expectancy, goal significance, friction reduction, effort evaluation.
- Out of scope: Detailed project scheduling techniques.
- Core questions: Why do people hold back effort on clear goals? How does friction reduce motivation?
- Units: NT-LAW-0029, NT-PRINCIPLE-0052, NT-PRINCIPLE-0058, NT-MODEL-0004, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0008.

### MOT-CL-002 — intrinsic-and-extrinsic-drive

- Definition: How internal ownership interacts with external rewards and surveillance.
- In scope: Autonomy, mastery, crowding-out effect, intrinsic satisfaction.
- Out of scope: Detailed compensation design models.
- Core questions: When do external rewards undermine intrinsic interest? How can autonomy be protected?
- Units: NT-LAW-0030, NT-PRINCIPLE-0053, NT-PRINCIPLE-0057, NT-MODEL-0005, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0005.

### MOT-CL-003 — fear-and-urgency

- Definition: How threat signals alter effort direction, urgency, and long-term commitment.
- In scope: Fear-driven urgency, defensive compliance, psychological pressure, commitment erosion.
- Out of scope: Emergency crisis management procedures.
- Core questions: What is the long-term cost of managing by fear? How can urgency be managed safely?
- Units: NT-LAW-0031, NT-PRINCIPLE-0054, NT-PRINCIPLE-0059, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0006.

### MOT-CL-004 — identity-and-persistence

- Definition: How self-concept and shared values sustain long-term commitment.
- In scope: Identity alignment, value fit, professional craft pride, long-term persistence.
- Out of scope: Personality classification taxonomies.
- Core questions: Why is identity-driven effort more durable than command compliance?
- Units: NT-LAW-0032, NT-PRINCIPLE-0055.

### MOT-CL-005 — progress-and-momentum

- Definition: How visible milestone feedback renews motivation and prevents burnout.
- In scope: Progress feedback, small wins, momentum loops, milestone visibility.
- Out of scope: KPI calculation formulas.
- Core questions: How does unacknowledged work erode motivation? How do small wins build momentum?
- Units: NT-LAW-0033, NT-PRINCIPLE-0056, NT-MODEL-0006, NT-ANTI-PATTERN-0007.
