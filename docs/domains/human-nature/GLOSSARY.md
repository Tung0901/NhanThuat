# Human Nature Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0001  
**Status:** frozen

- **Attention:** the limited human capacity to notice, prioritize, and sustain
  focus on selected signals.
- **Belonging:** the felt relationship between a person and a group, role, or
  shared identity.
- **Cognitive load:** the mental effort required to hold, process, decide, or
  act on information.
- **Adaptation:** a learned adjustment in expectation, emotion, habit, or
  behavior based on repeated context and consequence.
- **Defense:** protective behavior used to preserve safety, dignity, identity,
  or control under perceived threat.
- **Dignity:** the minimum respect owed to a person independent of performance,
  agreement, rank, or usefulness.
- **Energy:** the practical capacity to act, regulate emotion, decide, and
  sustain effort.
- **Emotion:** a felt and bodily readiness that organizes attention,
  interpretation, and possible action.
- **Identity:** the story a person protects about who they are, what they value,
  and where they belong.
- **Meaning:** the interpretation that makes an event, task, role, or cost feel
  significant.
- **Reinforcement/consequence:** the repeated reward, cost, ease, friction, or
  response that teaches people what behavior is worth repeating.
- **Salience:** the quality that makes a signal stand out as noticeable,
  urgent, meaningful, or relevant.
- **Safety:** enough protection from humiliation, punishment, or abandonment for
  truth and learning to remain possible.
- **Self-regulation:** the capacity to pause, choose, restrain, redirect, or
  sustain action under pressure.
- **Somatic/body signal:** a physical cue such as fatigue, tension, agitation,
  heaviness, or shutdown that may indicate load or readiness.
- **Status:** perceived standing, respect, importance, or influence within a
  relationship or group.
- **Threat:** a perceived risk to safety, dignity, control, belonging, status,
  or valued identity.
