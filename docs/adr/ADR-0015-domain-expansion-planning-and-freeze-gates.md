# ADR-0015: Domain Expansion Planning And Freeze Gates

**Status:** accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Deciders:** Product Owner

## Context

The Knowledge Factory provides workflow, quality gates, review findings, duplicate
and conflict candidate reporting, human approval requirements, and freeze
eligibility checks. Milestone 2 needs to apply that factory discipline to
domain-based expansion so future content batches remain reviewable and
traceable.

## Decision

Propose that each domain expansion batch declare its domain areas, concept
clusters, expected unit mix, dependency inputs, duplicate/conflict watchlist,
evidence needs, review owner, completeness criteria, and freeze target before
content authoring begins.

Domain completeness should be judged by balanced coverage, clear boundaries,
relation integrity, evidence posture, duplicate/conflict disposition, and review
evidence rather than unit count alone. Expected unit mix is a planning guardrail,
not a quota.

Waves and batches are distinct. Waves group related domain areas for planning;
batches are review-capacity-sized work packages inside a wave. Dependencies
inside a wave must be ordered before authoring.

Freeze remains a separate Product Owner-approved task. A domain blueprint may be
frozen separately from domain content. Domain content must pass its own
validation, tests, review, and governance gates before freeze.

Future implementation must define atomic batch/member freeze semantics before
domain content batches are frozen. The policy must state whether a frozen batch
requires all member records to be frozen, how aggregate and member statuses are
validated, and how frozen-register checks apply to non-Epic entries such as
batches, milestones, domain areas, and evidence sets.

Review owners may conduct domain review and recommend disposition, but they do
not approve or freeze unless they are acting as the Product Owner. Evidence
thresholds must distinguish process evidence from external support for
behavioral claims.

## Consequences

- Large domain batches remain constrained by review capacity.
- Completeness is quality-oriented instead of volume-oriented.
- Duplicate and conflict risks are surfaced before content generation.
- Evidence needs can be planned before external citations are available.
- No domain content or knowledge units are generated by this architecture
  decision.
- A future validator change is required before non-Epic frozen-register entries
  can be enforced with the same rigor as Epic entries.
- Blueprint completeness, catalogue completeness, content coverage, and freeze
  readiness remain separate gates.
