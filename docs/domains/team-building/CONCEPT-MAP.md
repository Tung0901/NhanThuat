# Team Building Concept Map

The Team Building domain (NT-DA-0006) focuses on the structural composition and interdependence of groups, turning a collection of individuals into a cohesive unit.

## Core Ontology

- **Cognitive Diversity**: The variance in trait defaults, problem-solving approaches, and perspectives within a group.
- **Structural Interdependence**: The degree to which members must rely on each other to achieve their goals.
- **Cohesion-Friction Curve**: The predictable dynamic where diverse teams experience high initial friction that resolves into high long-term cohesion and resilience.

## Conceptual Boundaries

- Differentiated from **Trust** (NT-DA-0010) and **Conflict** (NT-DA-0011): Team Building provides the *structure* within which trust and conflict occur, but does not dictate the emotional dynamics themselves.
- Differentiated from **Communication** (NT-DA-0008): Team Building establishes *who* must communicate and *why* (interdependence), not *how*.
