# Communication Domain Architecture

## Scope

The Communication domain establishes the foundational laws of information transfer, serving as the required substrate for all other domains (Delegation, Team Building, Hiring) without absorbing their unique mechanics.

## Ethical Safeguards

Communication must not be used as an organizational scapegoat. Structural, ethical, or goal misalignments must not be gaslit or dismissed as "miscommunications."

## Design Decisions

- Defines communication purely through the lens of information theory and cybernetics (Closed-Loop Transmission).
- Explicitly rejects "emotional intelligence," "persuasion," and "public speaking" from this domain to preserve strict ontology boundaries. Influence belongs to Leadership; Emotional Intelligence belongs to Human Nature/Personality.
