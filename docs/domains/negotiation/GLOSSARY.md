# Negotiation Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0012  
**Slug:** negotiation  
**Status:** frozen  

### BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement)
The most advantageous course of action a party can take if negotiations fail and no agreement is reached. BATNA sets the reservation value.

### Integrative Negotiation
A negotiation approach focused on expanding the pool of total value by discovering complementary interests before allocating resources (win-win).

### Interest
The underlying need, desire, fear, or concern that motivates a party's stance. Interests explain *why* a party wants something.

### Objective Criteria
Fair, independent benchmarks (market rates, scientific standards, legal precedents) used to determine agreement terms without personal bias.

### Position
The specific demand, dollar figure, or stance a party publicly insists upon. Positions obscure underlying interests.

### Reservation Value
The absolute walk-away point in a negotiation, determined by the party's BATNA.

### Stable Agreement
A negotiated contract structured such that all parties have a higher incentive to fulfill the commitment than to breach or default.

### ZOPA (Zone of Possible Agreement)
The overlap range between the minimum acceptable terms of both parties where an agreement can take place.
