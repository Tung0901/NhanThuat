# Conflict Glossary

**Domain Area:** NT-DA-0011  
**Slug:** conflict  
**Status:** frozen

### Task Conflict
Disagreement over perspectives, facts, or strategy choices regarding work execution.

### Relationship Conflict
Personal animosity, threat perception, or interpersonal friction directed at individuals.

### Artificial Harmony
The suppression of honest disagreement out of fear, resulting in passive aggression and poor choices.
