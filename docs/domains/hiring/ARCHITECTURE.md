# Hiring Domain Architecture

## Scope

The Hiring domain establishes laws and principles to mitigate bias, reduce false precision, and improve the structural matching of individuals to organizational roles.

## Ethical Safeguards

Hiring practices must preserve dignity and avoid algorithmic determinism. Assessments must be transparent and tied exclusively to role requirements, preventing discriminatory or arbitrary exclusion based on vague "culture fit" metrics.

## Design Decisions

- Rejects unstructured intuition as a valid selection mechanism.
- Emphasizes structural alignment over theoretical potential.
- Treats psychometric tools as diagnostic hypotheses rather than deterministic filters.
