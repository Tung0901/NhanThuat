How do people perceive, process, remember, and make sense of information under cognitive limits?

## Unit Inventory

*   **NT-LAW-3002 — Attention is Finite and Directional** (Law)
    - Attention is a strictly limited resource that can only be directed sequentially, making multitasking a myth of rapid context-switching with high switching costs.
*   **NT-LAW-3003 — Memory Reconstruction** (Law)
    - Memory is not a recording retrieved from storage, but a narrative reconstructed at the moment of recall, heavily influenced by present context and emotion.
*   **NT-MODEL-3001 — Information Processing Pipeline** (Model)
    - A structural model of how humans perceive, filter, process, and act upon environmental information.
*   **NT-MODEL-3002 — Sensemaking Loop** (Model)
    - The iterative process by which individuals and groups structure the unknown to enable action.
*   **NT-MODEL-3003 — Metacognitive Regulation Loop** (Model)
    - The structural model of how humans monitor, evaluate, and adjust their own cognitive processes and strategies during complex tasks.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3001 — Illusion of Explanatory Depth** (Phenomenon)
    - The false belief that one understands a complex system deeply, which collapses only when forced to explain the mechanics step-by-step.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3002 — Confirmation Bias Trap** (Phenomenon)
    - The tendency to eagerly accept information that confirms prior beliefs while rigidly scrutinizing or ignoring disconfirming evidence.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3003 — Availability Heuristic Fallacy** (Phenomenon)
    - Estimating the frequency or probability of an event based on how easily examples come to mind, leading to massive risk miscalculation.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3004 — Overconfidence Illusion** (Phenomenon)
    - The structural tendency for individuals to be more confident in their judgments and predictions than their objective accuracy warrants.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3005 — Ambiguity Aversion** (Phenomenon)
    - Humans consistently prefer known risks over unknown uncertainties, often choosing inferior options to avoid ambiguity.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3006 — Anchoring Effect** (Phenomenon)
    - The first piece of information offered in a decision context exerts a disproportionate influence on all subsequent judgments and estimates.
*   **NT-PHENOMENON-3007 — Metacognitive Incompetence Illusion** (Phenomenon)
    - Humans are inherently poor at accurately evaluating their own incompetence, because the skills required to perform a task are the exact same skills required to evaluate performance on that task.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3001 — Optimize Cognitive Load** (Principle)
    - Design environments, communications, and processes to minimize extraneous cognitive load, freeing capacity for intrinsic problem-solving.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3002 — Explicit Mental Modeling** (Principle)
    - Require individuals and teams to make their underlying assumptions and mental models explicit before making strategic decisions or resolving conflicts.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3003 — Foster Metacognitive Reflection** (Principle)
    - Design workflows to include mandatory structural pauses that force teams and individuals to step out of execution and evaluate their cognitive approach.
*   **NT-PRINCIPLE-3004 — De-bias through Structured Evaluation** (Principle)
    - Do not rely on willpower or awareness to overcome cognitive bias; instead, build structured environments and checklists that force objective evaluation.
