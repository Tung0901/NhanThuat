# Team Building Domain Architecture

## Scope

The Team Building domain establishes laws and principles for composing groups and structuring their workflows to demand collaboration, maximizing the value of cognitive diversity.

## Ethical Safeguards

Team building must prioritize psychological safety over forced assimilation. Boundaries of the "in-group" must be based on shared purpose and structural goals, never on demographic homogeneity or exclusionary prejudice.

## Design Decisions

- Defines a "team" strictly by structural interdependence, rejecting the colloquial use of the word for any group sharing a manager.
- Explicitly delegates trust and conflict resolution mechanics to future domains (NT-DA-0010, NT-DA-0011) to maintain strict ontology boundaries and avoid duplication.
